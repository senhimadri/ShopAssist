# Northwind Gear Returns Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The clock starts on the delivery date shown on the order, not the order date.

## Condition for returns
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories. Tents and sleeping bags may be returned only if the packaging seal is intact.

## Non-returnable items
Camp stoves, fuel canisters, and personal care items cannot be returned for safety and hygiene reasons unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item back. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the return was caused by our error.

# Northwind Gear Warranty Policy

## Coverage
Every product carries a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, misuse, and damage from weather events are not covered.

## Making a claim
To make a warranty claim, the customer must provide the order number and a photo of the defect. Claims are reviewed within 2 business days.

# Northwind Gear Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for an extra fee.

## Lost parcels
If a parcel shows as delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer must report it within 7 days of the delivery date. We then open a carrier investigation.
